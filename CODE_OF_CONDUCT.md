# Code of conduct

Awtrix Studio is a small hobby project. Everyone is welcome here, whatever their experience, background, or how well they speak English (it isn't the maintainer's first language either).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a friendly, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people writing in a second language.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding.
- Give and accept constructive feedback about code and ideas, never about people.
- Credit the work of others.
- Focus on what is best for the people who use the integration.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal or political attacks, and trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome advances.
- Harassment, in public or in private.
- Publishing someone else's private information (such as a real name, address or email) without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a public, welcoming space.

## Scope

This code applies in every space of the project: issues, pull requests, discussions, and wherever someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior bothers you, you can:

- mention **@3615nulsi** in a short message asking to talk privately, without repeating the details in public;
- or use GitHub's **Report content** feature (the ⋯ menu on any comment), which goes to GitHub's moderators.

Reports are handled discreetly, and the person who reports is protected from retaliation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, commits, issues and pull requests that break this code, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban a contributor, depending on how serious and repeated the behavior is.
